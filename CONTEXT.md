# Data Lab

Data Lab is a data engineering consultancy. This site markets its services to businesses by where they are in their data journey, and shows past client work.

## Language

**Journey Stage**:
One of three phases of a business's data maturity (Starting with Data, Scaling Operations, Optimizing & Governing). Services are grouped under exactly one stage.
_Avoid_: phase, level, tier

**Service**:
A consulting offering Data Lab sells, such as Cloud Migration or FinOps & Cost Optimization. Each belongs to one Journey Stage.
_Avoid_: product, package

**Solution**:
A case study of a past engagement, described as a Challenge, the Solution delivered, and its Outcome.
_Avoid_: project, case, portfolio item

**Industry**:
The client sector a Solution was delivered in (E-commerce, Financial Services, Healthcare), used to filter Solutions.
_Avoid_: sector, vertical
